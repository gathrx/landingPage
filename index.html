How it works

Retrieval-augmented
video

VideoRag applies retrieval-augmented generation to video: index everything once, retrieve only what matters, and let a language model answer from the evidence — not from guesswork.

01
Connect

Plug into the cameras and VMS you already run, or upload recorded archives. No new hardware.

RTSPONVIFVMSMP4
02
Index

Vision-language models describe every scene — people, vehicles, actions, text, attributes — and store them as searchable embeddings on a timeline.

VLMembeddingsOCR
03
Retrieve

Your question is matched against the index across all cameras and time ranges, pulling back only the clips that matter.

vector searchre-rank
04
Answer

An LLM reasons over the retrieved evidence and replies in plain language — every claim linked to a camera and timestamp you can play back.

Built for

Every team that
watches video

Security, operations and investigations all have the same problem: too much footage, too few people. VideoRag fits wherever cameras already are.

Public safety & law enforcement

Locate suspects, vehicles and evidence across city camera networks — and reconstruct incidents in minutes, not weeks.

“grey hatchback with a dented rear door, Tuesday after 9 PM”
Retail & loss prevention

Spot concealment, repeat offenders and internal shrink across every store without adding staff.

“someone putting items into a backpack in aisle 4”
Manufacturing & logistics

Catch unsafe actions, restricted-area entry and misplaced goods before they become downtime.

“worker without a helmet near the press line”
Airports, rail & transit

Find unattended bags, crowd build-up and access breaches without slowing passengers down.

“unattended suitcase near platform 2 for over 5 minutes”
Campuses & enterprise

Detect tailgating and after-hours activity, and support HR and security investigations instantly.

“two people entering on one badge swipe”
Media & video archives

Make years of recorded content searchable by what happens in it, not by file names and tags.

“every clip where the crowd stands up and cheers”
